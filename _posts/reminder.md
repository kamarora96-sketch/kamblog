subtitle: "Read this when you forget"

Most of the things I worry about never actually happen. The awkward email I spent an hour drafting? Nobody noticed. The project I was sure would fail? It didn't.

The conversation I rehearsed in my head? Went fine. I'm writing this down so future me can read it on a hard day: **you're probably overthinking it.**
