---
title: "Three things that worked this week"
subtitle: "Quick lessons, no fluff"
date: 2026-01-08
---

First, I batched similar tasks together instead of switching contexts all day. My focus lasted longer and I finished the week with energy to spare.

Second, I stopped refreshing my email every ten minutes — turned off notifications and only checked at set times. The world didn't end.

Third, I wrote down the one thing I wanted to finish before lunch, every morning. Sounds basic, but it changed how my days felt.
